# Weapons of the World

## Hero's Blade

A sacred sword category. The most famous is the one wielded by Link, blessed by Hylia. Others include:
- The Dawnblade (lost in the Goblin Wars)
- The Moonpiercer (wielded by Captain Seraphina)

## Cursed Blades

Dark mirrors of sacred weapons. They drain life force and corrupt their wielders over time.
