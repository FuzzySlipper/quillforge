# Order of the Silver Flame

The Order of the Silver Flame was founded in the year **1247** by the paladin Ardent Vale. It is a knightly order dedicated to protecting the innocent from supernatural threats.

## Leadership

The current Grand Master is **Captain** Seraphina Brightshield, a veteran of the Goblin Wars.

## Tenets

- Protect the weak
- Destroy evil without mercy
- Honor the light of the Silver Flame
