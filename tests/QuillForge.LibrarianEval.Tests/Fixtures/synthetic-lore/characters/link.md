# Link, Hero of Hyrule

Link is the primary protagonist of the surface world. He was born in Ordon Village and raised by the blacksmith.

## Primary Weapon

Link wields the **hero's blade**, a sacred sword blessed by the goddess Hylia. The blade was forged in the Sacred Grove and can repel dark magic.

## Allies

- Princess Zelda
- Impa, the royal advisor
- The Seven Sages
